# Design — Quote Line-art (`card-quote-lineart`)

**Lane:** Quote / line-art. **Format:** single static. **Primary ratio:** 1:1 (1080×1080). Also 4:5, 9:16, 1.91:1.
**Seeds:** Designs 4 (dark) & 5 (light) of the 2023 PSD kit — one template, a `theme` lever.
**Best for:** a borrowed line worth keeping, a brand line, a seasonal sentiment.
**Avoid for:** product drops, posts needing a photo or CTA.

A centred Lust quote breathing on an oversized line-art floral field. Light theme: ink on white, line-art at 15% ink. Dark theme: white on ink, line-art at 20% white. The attribution is Cervanttis, **its own block beneath the quote** — the system's voice-line rule, not an inline italic.

## Grid

- Quote centred, max-width 76% (60% landscape); Lust 52px / 1.35 (56 story, 38 landscape).
- Attribution Cervanttis 36px, 40px below the quote.
- Two line-art blooms, oversized and cropped at opposite corners (top-left upright, bottom-right rotated ~150°).

## Tokens

**cover** — `quote` (text), `attribution` (optional); lever `theme: light|dark`

## Voice discipline

- Sentence case, lowercase ease — the seed's "human connections are deeply nurtured…" is the register.
- One quote per post. If it needs two sentences, it should earn them.
- Attribution names the human: "Jean Houston". No title strings, no dashes.
- Chrome-free lane. AU English for brand lines; borrowed quotes stay as written.
